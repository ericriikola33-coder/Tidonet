How many hours of work does a Tesla Model 3 cost?

1,850 hours

At $20 an hour after tax. That is 231.2 work days of 8 hours.

A car is one of the biggest purchases most people make after a home. Even an entry-level electric car costs several months of full-time work, before insurance, charging, tires and repairs.

A smarter way to look at it

Remember the total cost of owning a car, not just the price. Insurance, maintenance and the money lost when the car loses value all add more work hours every single year.

Questions

How is this calculated?

Price divided by your take-home pay per hour. A work day is counted as 8 hours.

Where does the price come from?

US starting price of the base rear-wheel-drive Model 3 in 2026, before fees, taxes and options. Prices change, so you can type your own number in the calculator.

Is this the price I would pay?

Probably not exactly. Destination fees, taxes, options and local incentives change the final price. Type your own number in the calculator.
