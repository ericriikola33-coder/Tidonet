7 checks before you buy

  • Was it on your list before the sale? If not, it is not a saving. It is spending.
  • Check the price history. Some stores raise prices weeks before the sale, so the "discount" brings it back to normal. Price-history sites and browser extensions show you the real story.
  • Compare with the lowest recent price, not the "was" price on the tag.
  • Turn the price into hours of work. A $300 TV at $20 an hour is 15 hours of your life.
  • Wait 24 hours. If you still want it tomorrow, it is probably a real need. Most deals come back on Cyber Monday or in January anyway.
  • Watch out for "buy now, pay later". Splitting a purchase into four payments makes it feel smaller. It isn't.
  • Set a total budget for the week and stop when it is used up, however good the next deal looks.

Larry's math: "I saved $300!" by spending $900 on things he didn't need. Bella's math: one thing from her list, bought after 24 hours, and the rest of the money stays in her savings.

A good Black Friday purchase

It was on your list, you would buy it at full price anyway, and the price is lower than its lowest price over the past month. Then Black Friday is a great time to buy it.
