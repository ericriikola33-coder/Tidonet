About Tidonet

The name comes from tid, the Swedish word for time. That is the whole idea behind the site: time and money are connected, and seeing that clearly helps you make better choices.

Tidonet offers free, simple calculators that answer everyday questions:

  • How many hours do I have to work to buy this?
  • What can a small monthly amount grow into over the years?
  • What does a small daily habit really cost me?

The site is made by Nostalgicme, a channel that explains money simply in short videos on YouTube and TikTok. Tidonet is based in Gothenburg, Sweden.

Important to know

Everything on Tidonet is for learning and inspiration only. It is not financial advice. The calculators use simple, fixed assumptions, and real investments can lose value. If you are making a big financial decision, consider talking to a qualified advisor.
