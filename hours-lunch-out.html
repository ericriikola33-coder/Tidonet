How many hours of work does buying lunch every workday cost?

195 hours a year

At $20 an hour after tax. That is 45 minutes of work every workday.

Buying lunch on workdays is convenient, and it is also one of the larger habits in many budgets. Five days a week, all year, the hours add up fast.

Questions

How is this calculated?

Price divided by your take-home pay per hour. For a recurring cost, the yearly amount is the price every workday times 260.

Where does the price come from?

An example lunch price. Type what your lunch usually costs. Prices change, so you can type your own number in the calculator.

How many workdays does this assume?

About 260 workdays a year, which is five days a week. Fewer office days means fewer hours.
