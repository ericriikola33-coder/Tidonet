Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit tidonet.com and how it is used. Tidonet is run from Gothenburg, Sweden. Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page.

What you type in the calculators

All calculations happen in your own browser. The numbers you enter are not sent to us or stored on any server.

Information stored on your device

We save your chosen currency in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it next time. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser data.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can read more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your consent

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before personalised ads and related cookies are used. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time through the privacy settings link shown on the site.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, and to object to its use. Since we do not collect personal data ourselves, most data relating to ads is handled by Google. You also have the right to complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
