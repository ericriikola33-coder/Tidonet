How many hours of work does a PS5 cost?

32 hours

At $20 an hour after tax. That is 4.1 work days of 8 hours.

Consoles used to get cheaper over time. The PS5 has gone the other way, with several price increases since launch. Add a few games and an extra controller, and the real cost of gaming climbs even higher.

Questions

How is this calculated?

Price divided by your take-home pay per hour. A work day is counted as 8 hours.

Where does the price come from?

US price of the standard PS5 with a disc drive since April 2026. Prices change, so you can type your own number in the calculator.

Does this include games?

No. This is the console only. Games, subscriptions and accessories come on top, so the total number of work hours is usually higher.
