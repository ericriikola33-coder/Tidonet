12 hours

At $20 an hour after tax. That is 1.6 work days of 8 hours.

Questions

How is this calculated?

Price divided by your take-home pay per hour. A work day is counted as 8 hours.

Where does the price come from?

US list price of AirPods Pro 3, before sales tax. Prices change, so you can type your own number in the calculator.

How do I lower the cost?

Keep them longer, protect them with a case, and check whether a cheaper model covers what you actually need.
