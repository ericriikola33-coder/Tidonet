Questions

How is this calculated?

Price divided by your take-home pay per hour. A work day is counted as 8 hours.

Where does the price come from?

Average US price in The Economist's Big Mac Index, January 2026. Prices change, so you can type your own number in the calculator.

Why does the price differ where I live?

Prices vary by state and restaurant. Type the price on your local menu into the calculator for your own answer.
