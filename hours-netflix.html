How many hours of work does Netflix cost?

12 hours a year

At $20 an hour after tax. That is 1.0 hours of work a month.

Streaming feels cheap because the bill is small and arrives automatically. Over a year, a single subscription quietly adds up to many hours of work.

A smarter way to look at it

Rotate your subscriptions. Keep one service at a time, watch what you want, then cancel and switch. You keep the shows and cut the hours.

Questions

How is this calculated?

Price divided by your take-home pay per hour. For a recurring cost, the yearly amount is the price a month times 12.

Where does the price come from?

US price of the Standard plan without ads after the 2026 price increase. Prices change, so you can type your own number in the calculator.

Is the plan with ads worth it?

If ads do not bother you, a cheaper plan cuts the yearly work hours a lot. Try the calculator with the lower price to compare.
