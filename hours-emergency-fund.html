How many hours of work does a $1,000 emergency fund cost?

50 hours

At $20 an hour after tax. That is 6.2 work days of 8 hours.

Not every number on this site is about spending. A small emergency fund is often the first step to feeling calmer about money, because a surprise bill no longer turns into debt.
