How many hours of work does YouTube Premium cost?

9.6 hours a year

At $20 an hour after tax. That is 48 minutes of work a month.

YouTube Premium removes ads and adds background play. Like every subscription, the monthly price is small, but it keeps coming back year after year.

Questions

How is this calculated?

Price divided by your take-home pay per hour. For a recurring cost, the yearly amount is the price a month times 12.

Where does the price come from?

US price of the individual ad-free plan after the 2026 price increase. Prices change, so you can type your own number in the calculator.

Is there a cheaper option?

There is a lighter plan with fewer features at a lower price. Use the calculator to compare the two in work hours.
