How many hours of work does an iPhone 18 Pro cost?

60 hours

At $20 an hour after tax. That is 7.5 work days of 8 hours.

The iPhone 18 Pro is one of the most common big purchases people make, and many buy a new one every two or three years. A price tag over a thousand dollars is easy to accept when it is split into monthly payments. Measured in work hours, it looks very different.

A smarter way to look at it

Before upgrading, ask how many hours your current phone still has in it. Keeping a phone one extra year saves the full price spread over a longer time, and the money you did not spend can start growing instead.

Questions

How is this calculated?

Price divided by your take-home pay per hour. A work day is counted as 8 hours.

Where does the price come from?

US starting price for the 256 GB model at launch in September 2026, before sales tax. Prices change, so you can type your own number in the calculator.

Is it cheaper to pay monthly?

Monthly payments do not change the total price, they only spread it out. Some plans add interest or lock you into a contract, so the real cost can end up higher than the sticker price.
