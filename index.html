Questions

How is the growth calculated?

The calculator adds your monthly amount at the end of each month and applies the yearly return divided by twelve every month. That is called monthly compounding. Real investments go up and down, so the result is an estimate, not a promise.

What return should I use?

Many people use 5–8% a year as a long-term example for a broad stock index fund, but past returns do not guarantee future returns. Try a lower number to see a more careful scenario.

Should I use my salary before or after tax?

Use what actually lands in your bank account per hour, after tax. That is the money you really spend, so it gives the most honest answer.

Do you save what I type?

No. Everything is calculated in your own browser. The only thing remembered is your currency choice, stored on your own device. Read more in our privacy policy.
