How many hours of work does $1,500 rent cost?

900 hours a year

At $20 an hour after tax. That is 75 hours of work a month.

For most people, housing is the biggest cost of all. Seeing rent in work hours shows how much of every month goes to simply having a place to live.

A smarter way to look at it

A common rule of thumb is to keep housing around a third of your take-home pay or less. If it takes much more of your work hours, everything else gets tighter.

How is this calculated?

Price divided by your take-home pay per hour. For a recurring cost, the yearly amount is the price a month times 12.

What share of my pay should go to rent?
